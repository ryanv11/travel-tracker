DECIDED
Identity is CARRIED, not DERIVED. A nullable, namespaced cities.place_ref TEXT supplied in
the create request because the user picked a specific row — known before the insert, written
once, never touched by the geocoder. Coordinates leave the identity question entirely. F1-F5
dissolve rather than get patched: all five are properties of using coordinates as identity,
not of the bucket size.

  attacked — 8 probes, incl. two unfavourable results reported (T5, T7).

THE ONE-LINE FRAMING CORRECTION
"cities.json has no stable row id" is TRUE. "cities.json has no identity" is FALSE — everyone
inferred the second from the first, and the coordinate bucket exists entirely because of it.
It has no id COLUMN; it has a natural key. The app discards admin2, which is exactly what
separates the four Newports (E1/O2/E4/G2).
