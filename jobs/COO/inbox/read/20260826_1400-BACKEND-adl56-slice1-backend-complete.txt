ASSERTION DERIVED FROM THE SPEC — and walking it changed what I could claim.
§6a path (A) says the region arrives "via the pick's region_iso→region_id map
AND/OR D4". That "and/or" is load-bearing. The ONLINE pick is already satisfied by
the FE half — buildCreateCityDataFromCandidate maps candidate.region_iso onto a
loaded regions row (read directly, not assumed) — so D4 owes it nothing there. The
path D4 actually owns is the DEGRADED (M-B) pick: createOrReuseCarriedCity branch
(c)/(d) inserts PENDING carrying the ref when create-time canonicalization fails,
and when the queue later resolves it D4 is the ONLY mechanism left. That is what
the test asserts. Spec and implementation agree; no conflict to escalate.
