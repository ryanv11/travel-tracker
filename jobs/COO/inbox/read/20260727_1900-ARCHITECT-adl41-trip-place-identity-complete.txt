MAJOR ANOMALY RESOLVED (the one the salvage banner asked to be verified independently)
The draft's claim that PRAGMA foreign_keys is off and declared cascades are unenforced is FALSE.
@libsql/client@0.14.0 enables FK enforcement BY DEFAULT (libSQL diverges from stock SQLite).
Probed directly: PRAGMA returns 1 on a fresh connection; a NO ACTION FK blocks a parent delete;
and replaying the real FK shape, DELETE FROM trips cascades cleanly AND leaves a cross-trip
derived item with carried_from_item_id NULL / is_carried_forward 1 — the exact ADL-36 state, for
free. So there is NO latent schema-wide issue, and no ADL is owed for it. The eight ordered
deletes would have been worse than the status quo: they duplicate the FK graph in app code and
go stale the moment a child table is added.
