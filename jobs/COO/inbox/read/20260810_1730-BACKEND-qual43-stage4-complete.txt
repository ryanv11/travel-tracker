COO ADDITIONS I'D CHALLENGE — one, and it's real
 - "assertOwned has no production caller; you are its first." I am not, and I did not manufacture
   one. Every assertion-guarded read in this stage already sits behind a STRONGER gate that itself
   composes scopeToUser: assertWritable on the write paths, a scoped findById/findByIdOrThrow on the
   read paths. Adding assertOwned would have meant a redundant SELECT per request to satisfy a
   checklist. It stays correct and unused; it is the right shape for a read-gate on a trip whose
   owner is not otherwise looked up, and no such site exists yet. Needs a decision: leave it as the
   documented seam, or retire it. I recommend LEAVE — it is named in scope.ts's contract, in the
   check script's remediation text, and in ADL-53 §2; deleting it would cost more than it saves.

reaches zero in the same PR that empties the files), and switch-by-switch Part F runs were cheap.
