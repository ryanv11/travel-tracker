## ADL-48 in one paragraph

Recommendation: **gazetteer-first, not gazetteer-only.** Bundle a local dataset as the primary
index; keep the geocoder for the tail. Decided on real Scottish place names, not population
theory: Glasgow/Edinburgh/Inverness/Ullapool/Portree present; Plockton, Applecross, Shieldaig,
Lochinver, Durness, Kyleakin, Gairloch, Dornie, Braemar absent from both candidate datasets.
BUG-30's own note makes the Scotland dogfood trial the trigger requirement — a gazetteer-only
build ships a product that cannot record the trip it was built for. Biggest technical catch:
GeoNames `admin1` is NOT ISO 3166-2 — correct for 2 of 26 region-tier countries, numeric for
20 — and would have written `AU-08` where the app expects `AU-NSW`, silently, passing every
test because GB and US are the only region-tier countries with seeded regions today.
