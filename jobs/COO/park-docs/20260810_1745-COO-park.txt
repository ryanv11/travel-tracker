## Two defects found and fixed IN our own governance machinery

- **OP-40 cadence gate (#486).** PO caught it: "isn't this session 77 and we already ran the
  audit?" The `session_end` counter had frozen (hook wired correctly, event stopped arriving —
  root cause upstream **UNVERIFIED**), AND `count % N` kept no record of whether a check had RUN,
  so the deep audit re-fired for three sessions. Rebuilt on the invariant *due = distance since
  last recorded run*, tick = max of three differently-failing sources, plus a wall-clock staleness
  backstop. The silent direction was the worse one: freeze on a non-multiple and the audit never
  fires again with nothing to say so.
- **QUAL-46 / ci-wait.sh (#493).** D-24 (filed 2026-08-04 as watch-if-recurs) RECURRED and is now
  a tracker entry; D-24 deleted from open-dialogues per delete-on-resolution. `ci-wait.sh` reported
  "PASS @ 617d6fa" when the head was 17b8cdf — a **fail-open on the primary CI gate**. Root cause
  read, not inferred: `:123` pins the head once, before the watch. Fixed by re-resolving after the
  watch and refusing a moved head. The post-merge main-CI check does NOT cover this case (it runs
  after the merge lands). Mutation-tested.
