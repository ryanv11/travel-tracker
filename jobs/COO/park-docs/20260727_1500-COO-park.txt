### 3. The most valuable output was a correction, not a decision
S1 was re-dispatched as a COMPLETION task pointed at the salvaged doc, told to review it
critically rather than rubber-stamp. It found two claims false, both already spread into
our own planning docs:

1. **"PRAGMA foreign_keys is off at runtime"** — FALSE. `@libsql/client` enables FKs by
   default (a libSQL divergence from stock SQLite), verified by direct probe. The draft's
   proposed eight ordered deletes would have been WORSE than the status quo: duplicating
   the FK graph in application code, going stale the first time a child table was added.
   No latent issue, no ADL owed. Residual: the probe covered the embedded driver only —
   the REMOTE Turso path is unverified (firewall blocks every Turso host). → QUAL-11.
2. **"There is no router.delete in trips.ts at all"** — FALSE. `tripsRouter.delete('/:id')`
   exists at trips.ts:429, userId-scoped, 403 on locked, cascading; `useDeleteTrip` already
   wired into both trip layouts. The original probe grepped `router.delete` against a
   router named `tripsRouter`, and **the COO propagated it into BUG-50's tracker note
   without re-verifying**. Corrected in plan §6.2 and the tracker. **B7 is frontend-only.**

S3 likewise rejected its own brief's framing on measured evidence: BUG-45 and OQ-06 are
NOT one mechanism. 22 countries have `region_tier_enabled=1` with zero seeded regions (22
latent BUG-30s), and the recommended dataset has the IDENTICAL gap that caused BUG-30 — so
a per-country override table is mandatory regardless of source.
