## Process note for future sessions
Three background agents hit an account session-limit cutoff mid-task simultaneously
(dispatched in parallel, all cut off in the same window). All three resumed cleanly via
SendMessage to their agentId with no lost work — the harness preserves transcript state
across the cutoff. Not a bug, just worth knowing: if a batch of parallel agents all fail
with "session limit" notifications, resume each one rather than re-dispatching from
scratch.

Also worth carrying forward: **a subagent's own "PR is up, CI passing" self-report is
not sufficient — verify by reading the actual CI job log yourself before merging.** The
OP-11 first-round PR had all *pre-existing* CI jobs green and would have looked mergeable
from a shallow check; only reading the new E2E job's own log revealed it was failing
30/36. This session is the concrete case study for that rule.
