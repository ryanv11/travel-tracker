WHAT THE BRIEF ASKED
  Rule on the MECHANISM for fixing BUG-71 (P1): how to give the ambiguity
  discriminator enough evidence to fire correctly without violating Nominatim's
  usage policy. Decision document, not code. Four candidate approaches offered
  (a) raise limit (b) two-phase (c) trip-country constraint / D-19 (d) something else.

WHAT WAS RULED — eleven decisions, all High confidence except one split
  B1  Invert the discriminator, don't feed it more. Confirm-or-ask. Zero extra requests.
  B2  Truncation is observable and free: rawCount >= limitSent, measured on the RAW array.
  B3  (a) ADOPT as an ENABLER, discovery call only, 10 -> 40. REJECTED as a fix on its own.
  B4  (b) REJECT — arithmetic in §6.1, but the decisive objection is that it doesn't
      remove the need for B1.
  B5  (c) REJECT as the BUG-71 fix, ENDORSE as the destination. Corrected the dispatch's
      premise: (c) costs ZERO extra requests.
  B6  Move the decision to the backend — new classifyDiscovery beside classifyCandidates.
  B7  F1/F2 §2.2 parity contract SUPERSEDED, honoured by deleting one side.
  B8  Never narrow the region selector on a truncated set.
  B9  Resolution path untouched; instrumented only; trigger recorded (§10.3).
  B10 YES, a frontend change is required — but the backend half is independently
      deployable and independently closes the violation.
  B11 No new requirement ID. GE-15's success criteria MUST be amended before UAT.

THE FINDING THAT REFRAMED THE WHOLE QUESTION
  The dispatch (correctly) described the limit:'10' global query as the mechanism.
  The deeper fault is that GET /api/geocode makes NO ambiguity decision at all — it
  calls nominatimSearch directly and hands back candidates[0]. F1/F2 §2.1's
  one-classifier instruction reached the two resolution call sites and stopped there.
  So the real discriminator for the flow the PO actually used is hand-rolled in a
  React component. That is why raising the limit cannot be the fix: there is nothing
  on the backend to raise the limit FOR.

THE DESIGN PROPERTY THE BRIEF DEMANDED, AND HOW IT WAS MET
  Requirement: "a ruling that only works if Nominatim is generous is not acceptable."
  The old rule needed Nominatim to VOLUNTEER a second region before it would ask the
  user — generosity was a precondition for correctness. The new rule needs a complete,
  fully attributed, unanimous set before it will answer FOR the user. The test I used
  to check this: enumerate every condition under which the new rule still auto-fills
  "Virginia" for "springfield". They all have to hold at once, and in that world
  Virginia genuinely is the only US Springfield Nominatim knows. It can only fail
  toward a right answer. No fact about the live payload is required.

IF SOMEONE PICKS THIS UP AND WANTS TO "SIMPLIFY" IT — three things that look like
tidy-ups and are not:
  1. DO NOT share DISCOVERY_LIMIT with resolveCityName/resolveCity (still 10).
     Raising those widens `eligible`, which flips ok->ambiguous and changes coordinate
     writes — a resolution-path behaviour change this ruling does not authorise (B9).
  2. DO NOT collapse 'none' and 'ambiguous' in DiscoveryConfidence. BUG-73 needs
     exactly that distinction for its three-state presentation and would have to
     reintroduce it.
  3. DO NOT remove the [GEO] raw/limit/truncated log line as noise. It is the only
     instrument that answers ENV-01 on this path and the only way we learn Nominatim's
     real effective cap.
  And one that looks like a contradiction and is not: classifyDiscovery being a SECOND
  classifier does not breach F1/F2 §2.1. That instruction forbids two procedures
  answering the SAME question. These answer different ones ("what may we pre-fill in a
  form" vs "may we write coordinates to a shared row"). §4.4 pins them with a testable
  strictly-stricter invariant so they cannot drift.
