THE ONE THING TO REMEMBER
-------------------------
The three services do NOT support "read-only" equally, and I did not pretend otherwise:
- Turso  = clean read-only exists (--read-only per-DB token) -> GRANT
- Railway = NO read-only scope (any token can deploy/delete) -> GRANT WITH CAVEAT
            (Project token = narrowest reach; read-only is behavioural discipline only)
- Clerk  = NO read-only credential (sk_* = full write to identity provider + all PII)
            -> DO NOT GRANT; keep manual dashboard/paste. Highest stakes.

Per the brief's instruction not to quietly widen scope: where read-only wasn't achievable
(Railway, Clerk) I flagged it as a finding rather than working around it. Clerk is a
recommend-decline; Railway is grant-with-eyes-open.
