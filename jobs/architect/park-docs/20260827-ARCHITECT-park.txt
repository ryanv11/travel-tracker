2. THE ARGUMENT, IN SHORT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The COO's "one pattern, not four incidents" framing is UPHELD, and the cheaper
alternative it offered (re-enumerate once more) is REJECTED — a second one-time
enumeration has the same half-life as the first.

But the COO's diagnosis is incomplete in a way that changes the fix. It said the
artifact rotted because a one-time list was used for an ongoing problem. The
deeper defect is that the RULE GENERATING THE LIST HAS NO STOPPING CONDITION.
"Enumerate every way the test environment differs from production" is not a
completable instruction — the difference set between vitest+jsdom and
Chrome-on-Railway is effectively infinite, and most of it is the entire reason the
tier is cheap. Nobody can be assigned an uncompletable task, so "give it an owner"
alone would not have worked.

The fix inverts it: enumerate each tier's FIDELITY CLAIMS (a short, closed list of
production properties it asserts it reproduces) plus its DECLARED NON-GOALS.
Everything on neither list is out-of-contract — an admitted unknown rather than a
silent gap. That is bounded, therefore ownable.

Second framing correction: "the test environment" is FIVE environments (T1 backend
integration, T2 frontend unit, T3 contract, T4 E2E, T5 shakedown), each a model of
something different. Fidelity is a per-tier contract, never a global property.
BUG-101 is a T1 defect; QUAL-18 is T4; QUAL-54 is a T4/T5 tooling property. They
were filed as though they were the same kind of thing.

5. THE MECHANISM, AND ITS HONEST LIMIT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
M2 is the load-bearing rule: A FIDELITY CLAIM IS AN EXECUTABLE ASSERTION, OR IT IS
NOT A CLAIM. Prose parity claims are banned. server-test-app.ts:1-11 is the
exemplar of the harm — a reader has no reason to re-check a file that asserts its
own parity. Each parity assertion is also demonstrated RED once, which is the only
cheap defence against a vacuous assertion (QUAL-22 one level up).

M3 extends OP-40's EXISTING machinery, as the brief required: one new Check F in
scripts/drift-canary.sh (F1 unbacked claim / F2 prose parity language / F3
green-by-suppression), on the existing drift-cadence slot. No new script, cadence
or job. It needs SCAN_DIRS widened to src/ and .github/ for F2/F3.

VERIFIED, not inherited: the COO's claim that the drift machinery could not have
caught these HOLDS, and the reason is drift-canary.sh's SCAN_DIRS/EXTS at lines
22-24 — it reads only .md/.txt/.json under .claude, _shared and jobs, plus
CLAUDE.md/CODEBASE.md. It never opens src/, .github/ or playwright.config.ts. A
scope limit, not a defect, and it is the seam M3 extends.

THE LIMIT, STATED IN THE ADL RATHER THAN HIDDEN: none of F1-F3 can detect a
divergence in a property NOBODY HAS EVER NAMED. QUAL-54 would NOT have been caught
by Check F, and neither would D-3. The mechanism closes the RECURRENCE of a named
divergence and the SILENT DECAY of a stated claim. That is a smaller promise than
the brief hoped for and it is the true one. M4's four human triggers exist for
precisely that residue.
