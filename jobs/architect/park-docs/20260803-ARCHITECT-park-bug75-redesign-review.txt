3. THE TWO BLOCKERS, IN ONE PARAGRAPH EACH
================================================================================
R1 — step 0d produces a WRONG TOWN, not just a duplicate. Step 4c creates a
'pending' row precisely when the geocoder verdict is AMBIGUOUS, i.e. when the name
has several real referents and the route deliberately declined to choose (D14).
Step 0d then adopts that row and stamps it with ONE specific place's place_ref and
coordinates. A Shropshire trip ends up pinned on the Isle of Wight and two trips
share one row — BUG-75, re-created by the fix. Also cross-user through the
permanent created_by_user_id IS NULL branch. The design's defence that this is
"inherited from D13 step 2, not widened" is false: D13 step 2 writes {regionId,
geocodeAttempts, updatedAt} — it fills a NULL region; step 0d writes {place_ref,
latitude, longitude, geocode_status} on a row whose region already matched — it
decides which town. FIX: delete step 0d, always insert. The design's own §3.3
severity ordering demands it.

R2 — E2 is not implementable. Step 0b dereferences gazetteer_cities, which does not
exist in this codebase (three independent probes). It is ADL-48 stage S2, which the
E-sequence never lists. "Inert until a client sends one" is not the property
claimed: a client can send one and gets a 500, not 0b's fail-closed 400. FIX:
insert E2a (ADL-48 S2) and record the ordering S1 -> S2 -> E2 -> E3.

6. SIX FINDINGS I KILLED BEFORE FILING
================================================================================
1. "admin2 will be sparse exactly where collisions are worst." FALSE — 92.43% of
   colliding rows carry a non-empty admin2, 100% in GB. This was my headline
   hypothesis and it would have hollowed out the design's best idea.
2. "Step 0 removes the geocoder's rate limit, so any user can bulk-publish into the
   global catalogue." FALSE — server.ts:186 applies a dedicated 20 req/min
   citiesCreateLimiter to POST /api/cities, independent of the global limiter.
3. "The COALESCE expression index won't be usable; the tail degrades to a scan."
   FALSE — EXPLAIN QUERY PLAN uses the expression term.
4. "The 0.2 degree tolerance inflates the 97.78% overlap." Non-issue, measured.
5. "A year of monthly releases compounds 0.475% into something ugly." FALSE —
   0.236%/rebuild, ~2.8%/yr, compounding validated against a held-out pair.
6. "Include coordinates only for rows in a residual collision group." Rejected on
   the design's own reasoning — a row can acquire a same-key sibling later, which
   would change its place_ref retroactively. The design's choice is right.
