IF YOU PICK THIS UP: THE FIVE THINGS THAT WILL BITE
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
1. NEVER let the cache's ANSWER become an input to useLiveCityLookup.
   "The cache returned one confident match, skip the lookup" IS BUG-97. It
   was designed in once (ADL-56 R1 §3b(3)) and caught only by a fresh-eyes
   review. Tests 1 and 10 are the regression tests; if you find yourself
   making either of them pass by another route, stop.

2. The NB-1 cue's `!searching` half is load-bearing, not tidiness.
   `live.pending && !searching`. The cue announces an augmentation still
   arriving — a claim that is only meaningful once the saved list is on
   screen, and the click-too-early race it warns about cannot happen while
   nothing is clickable. Remove it and NB-1's non-blocking test goes red
   for a genuine reason.

3. handleCommit awaits fetchCountryRegions() on purpose.
   The held candidate's country is only known at pick time, so the
   useCountryRegions hook may not have fetched it yet. Reading the hook
   instead races it and silently drops the region — which IS BUG-98's
   symptom ("Australia (no state set)").

4. N3 degrades a held live selection to 'plain'; it does not clear it.
   Same safety property (the osm_id cannot ride under a new country/name),
   better UX. Both would pass the tests. Don't "simplify" to clearing
   without reading §3a's N3 paragraph first.

5. The two escape rows are not redundant by accident.
   `add-place-none-of-these` selects in place; `+ Add new: "X"` navigates
   to the manual form. Merging them pre-satisfies the manual form's §3a
   guard and re-opens the Melbourne region-null save through that path —
   and nine existing suites depend on `+ Add new` opening the form.

The "manual form's picker still commits on the pick" boundary in the KNOWN /
DECLARED section above is CLOSED. It is converted. Read that section as
history, not current state.

Why it was wrong: I defined BUG-99's defect as "dates structurally
unreachable" and reasoned from my definition. The tracker defines it
differently and names the excluded surface by symbol — title "picking a result
from EITHER picker commits immediately", note "confirmed on BOTH add-place
pickers … handleSelectPickerCandidate ~:362 -> handleSelectCity ~:289", and the
commit bypasses dates "shown in the same view". Same-view reachability was
never an exemption. GE-21: "choosing a result populates the form but writes
nothing until the explicit Add."

Now: handleSelectPickerCandidate holds (formHeldCandidate) and writes nothing;
handleCreateCity is the only writer on that screen. Identity-carry logic
byte-identical — only its timing moved. Hold invalidated by name edit, country
change, "none of these", or a fresh lookup. Holding satisfies the §3a guard.

New [S1][GAP] block in AddPlaceFlow.adl56-select-not-commit.test.tsx pins it;
proven red by reverting the conversion (expected [Array(2)] to deeply equal [],
receiving ["/api/cities","/api/trips/1/places"]). The guard test in that block
stays green under the revert — correct, different rule.

SO THE STANDING RULE ON THIS SURFACE IS NOW SIMPLE: on AddPlaceFlow, NO pick
anywhere commits. Three pick sites (cached row, merged-surface live candidate,
manual-form picker candidate) all select; two commit controls exist (the search
screen's add-place-commit and the form's Add City & Place submit) and only they
write. If you add a fourth pick site, it selects.

STILL commit-on-pick, deliberately: ChangeCityModal (both its cached rows and
its live candidates). §12-Q5 excludes the held-selection/dates restructure
there — no dates, no "Add", so GE-21's clause has no referent. Flagged to COO
for confirmation rather than assumed.
