SUMMARY
================================================================================
Tiny, well-scoped copy fix from PO UAT. GeocodeQueueIndicator.tsx's collapsed
needs-attention badge and its aria-label rendered `{n} need attention` at
every count — grammatically wrong at n=1 ("1 need" should be "1 needs"). Fixed
per the PO's exact wording: "1 place needs attention" / "N places need
attention". No logic, data, or styling change.

================================================================================
THE FIX, EXACTLY
================================================================================
needsAttentionPhrase(n) = `${n} place${n===1?'':'s'} need${n===1?'s':''} attention`
  n=1 → "1 place needs attention"
  n=2 → "2 places need attention"

resolvingPhrase(n) = `${n} place${n===1?'':'s'} resolving`
  n=1 → "1 place resolving"
  n=2 → "2 places resolving"

aria-label = `Geocoding: ${needsAttentionPhrase(n1)}, ${resolvingPhrase(n2)}. Open queue.`
  e.g. "Geocoding: 1 place needs attention, 1 place resolving. Open queue."

================================================================================
RESOLVING-CHIP CONSISTENCY CALL (brief left this to my judgment)
================================================================================
The brief only mandated the needs-attention fix; the resolving chip ("N
resolving") was already grammatically correct at every count on its own — no
verb-agreement bug there. I chose to ALSO mirror the "place(s)" noun onto it
("N places resolving") rather than leave it bare, because:
- Once the needs-attention chip became a full noun phrase ("N places need
  attention"), the two chips sitting side-by-side in the same badge read as
  two different sentence shapes — one names what's counted, the other doesn't.
- The change is free: "1 place resolving" reads exactly as naturally as
  "1 resolving", and "N places resolving" reads better than "N resolving" next
  to the now-longer needs-attention phrase.
- It costs zero extra surface area — same two-line helper pattern, one more
  call site, no new component or styling.
If the PO prefers the shorter original "N resolving" (e.g. thinks the pairing
reads fine as-is or the extra word adds clutter), it's a one-line revert of the
resolvingPhrase call sites back to the raw count — flagging so this is an easy
undo, not a load-bearing design decision.
